- experience_misleading: one retrieved experience has a trigger that is too broad, or its guidance misled the model for this problem.
- strategy_misleading: the retrieved strategy itself is too broad, incomplete, or structurally misleading.
- experience_miss: the retrieved skills are not clearly wrong, but the skillbank lacks a needed local modeling warning or guidance; create a new experience.
- strategy_miss: the retrieved skills are not clearly wrong, but the skillbank lacks a needed global modeling strategy; create a new strategy.
- code_error_only: the mathematical model is basically correct, and the failure is mainly an implementation/code issue.
- unresolved: there is not enough evidence to safely update or add a skill.

- Only repair a skill when the debugging information reveals a real weakness in the skill content.

- A repaired strategy should preserve the same broad modeling family while clarifying missing structure.
- A repaired experience should refine the trigger and guidance.
- A new strategy should summarize a reusable modeling skeleton not covered by the retrieved strategy.
- A new experience should summarize a reusable trigger and modeling/code guidance not covered by the retrieved experiences.
- If the issue is code-only, do not produce a skill.
